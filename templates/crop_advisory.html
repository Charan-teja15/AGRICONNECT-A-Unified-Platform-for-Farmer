This assistant uses simple, transparent rules configured in the application. It is not a trained AI model and does not provide a laboratory diagnosis.

Tips for better guidance

  • Be specific: “yellow leaves near the base” beats “leaf problem”.
  • Mention when the problem started and how fast it spreads.
  • Note recent fertiliser, pesticide or rainfall events.
  • Include the field area affected, if you know it.

How the guidance is produced

  1. Your answers are matched against the advisory rules stored in the database.
  2. Each rule contains a condition, the guidance text and the reason for it.
  3. The result page shows the guidance and the rule that produced it.
  4. If nothing matches, you will be told so — no guess is invented.
Please note: advisory output is informational. For serious or spreading problems, contact your local agriculture officer or Krishi Vigyan Kendra before applying chemicals.
