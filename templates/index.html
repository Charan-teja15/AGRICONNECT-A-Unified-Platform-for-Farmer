How it works

Three simple steps

No training required — if you can fill a form, you can use AGRICONNECT.

1

Create your farmer profile

Register with your name, contact details, state, district and land details so the platform can personalise your results.

2

Enter your crop or farming information

Add a crop listing, describe your crop condition, or share your landholding details for scheme matching.

3

Explore relevant listings, advice, or scheme matches

Review results with clear explanations, then act on them with the confidence of knowing why they were suggested.

Why AGRICONNECT

Built for clarity, not complexity

Every screen is designed to be understandable at a glance — even for first-time smartphone users.

Three services in one place

Marketplace, crop advisory and subsidy awareness share a single account and profile.
